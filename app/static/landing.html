How it works

From a listed video to an approved, delivered placement

Every step is recorded, and every approval is tied to the exact version of the placement.

Creators list videos

Set a fixed price, the channels and territories you license, and the categories you never want in your content.

Brands find a fit

Create a campaign and see analysed scenes that match it, with clear reasons when something is blocked. Send a fixed-price offer.

Place the product

Pin a product image or a 3D model to a surface in the scene. The quality check tracks every frame and shows a preview from the production renderer.

Approve and deliver

The creator consents, both sides approve, and the render is checked frame by frame before download. Creators can take it down at any time.

Quality, honestly

We would rather refuse a shot than ship a bad placement

Placements are composited deterministically on surfaces tracked through the shot. Nothing is generated by AI, and every output is validated before anyone can download it.

SUPPORTED

Tracked 2D placement

Product images on flat surfaces through camera moves, with colour matched to lighting changes across the clip.

SUPPORTED

3D objects on a tracked plane

A .glb model standing on a real surface, with the camera solved from the footage, perspective-correct shading and contact shadows.

FAQ

Questions brands and creators ask first

How do I sign up?

Continue with Google, or enter your email and we'll send a one-time code and sign-in link. The first time, choose whether you are joining as a brand or a creator and name your workspace. If a teammate invited you, you can join their workspace instead.

Whose footage can be used?

Only footage the creator owns or is licensed to use. Brands only see a creator's video after the creator lists it, and only receive the stream once the creator accepts an offer.

Is the product generated by AI?

No. The product is your own image or 3D model, composited onto a surface tracked through the shot. Generative rendering is switched off until a provider passes a live verification.

How are payments handled?

Agreed fees are recorded on each offer and in the creator's earnings. Payment processing is not built in yet, so you settle fees directly for now.

Can a creator take a placement down?

Yes. Revoking a licence cancels pending renders, disables download links and blocks further delivery.

Ready to try it on your own content?

Early access is open to brands and creators. Start with a video you own or a product you sell.
