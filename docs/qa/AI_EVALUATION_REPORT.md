# AI evaluation report

> **Superseded.** This is the record as of before V4.0 M14. The current state is in [docs/engineering/FINAL_RELEASE_REPORT.md](../engineering/FINAL_RELEASE_REPORT.md) and, for the intelligence pipeline, [docs/engineering/AI_EVALUATION.md](../engineering/AI_EVALUATION.md).

**Measured:** 4 November 2026, 23:47, on this development machine (AMD Family 23, 8 logical processors, Windows 11, OpenCV 4.11, FFmpeg 8.1).
**Command:** `python -m benchmark.evaluate_intelligence --out benchmark/results/intelligence.json`.
**Raw results:** [benchmark/results/intelligence.json](../../benchmark/results/intelligence.json), including every clip.

> **What this does and does not show.** All footage is synthetic, generated by `benchmark/fixtures.py` and `benchmark/fixtures_scene.py`, with exact ground truth. The results show that the pipeline measures what it claims to on controlled input. They say **nothing about accuracy on real footage**. No licensed, labelled real footage exists yet (blocker B-7). No number here may be quoted as real-world accuracy.

## What the pipeline is
| Stage | Method | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Segmenting | Colour histograms (8×8×8 bins, 96×54 px) every 0.25 s, compared by Bhattacharyya distance. The distance is the smaller of (a) the distance as recorded and (b) the distance after scaling each sample to a mean brightness of 128 (gain 0.4× to 2.5×). A cut is called above 0.35. Scenes shorter than 1.0 s are merged | Measured below |
| Detecting objects, tracking objects | None | `UNAVAILABLE`: no model has been evaluated for commercial use (B-6) |
| Understanding scenes | Measured motion, brightness and colourfulness only | `PARTIAL`: no vision model configured |
| Detecting placements | `decisions.detect_surfaces` (plain planar regions), followed by re-detection every 0.5 s with IoU ≥ 0.5 | Measured below |
| Scoring | Deterministic weighted factors from `scoring_configs` v1. Unmeasured factors are left out and the weights rescaled | Ranking aid, not a validated prediction |

## Results

### Scene cuts
Ground truth: `cut_inside` clips have a cut at 1.6 s and `cut_after` clips one at 3.0 s. The other 12 kinds have no cut. 14 kinds × 3 seeds gives 42 clips. A detected cut within 0.25 s of a true cut counts as a true positive.

| | True positives | False positives | Missed | Precision | Recall |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Before the change (histogram as recorded) | 3 | 6 | 3 | 0.33 | 0.50 |
| Final (`cut_gain_normalise` = 1) | 3 | **0** | 3 | **1.00** | 0.50 |

- **The false positives before the change** were all in `mild_lighting` (±8% exposure drift) and `lighting_change` (brightness falling to about 50%): one false cut at 1.2 s in each of the six clips.
- **The misses, before and after,** are the three `cut_after` clips. Their cut is at 3.0 s in a 3.2 s clip, so the 0.2 s closing scene is shorter than `min_scene_seconds`, and `segment` merges it into the scene before by design. A sub-second tail cannot hold a placement. The metric counts these as misses, and so does this report.
- **A first attempt** compared only the normalised histogram. It fixed the lighting clips but made two of the three plain-wall `bare` clips split into two scenes. A flat frame scaled to a mean of 128 sits on a histogram bin edge. The final rule requires both distances to be high, because a real cut changes both. Per-sample maximum distances on the final rule: real cuts 0.54–0.61, every non-cut kind at most 0.33 (`motion_blur`), the bare clips 0.10–0.12.

### Placement surfaces
| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| Poster found (IoU ≥ 0.5 with ground truth) on the 42 tracking clips | **3 of 42 (7%)**. All three are the `low_texture` clips (IoU 0.90–0.91); the median best IoU is 0.0 |
| Room clips (wall poster and table) with at least one opportunity | 3 of 3, two opportunities each |
| Bare clips (no usable surface) with an opportunity | 0 of 3 |
| Every analysis completed | 48 of 48 |

`detect_surfaces` looks for plain, uniform planar regions such as walls, tables and plain boards. The tracking clips' poster is heavily textured, and the detector does not find it. That is a real limitation (technical debt 15), not a measurement error. The boxes on both sides are `(x0, y0, x1, y1)` in pixels, checked by hand on three clips. Posters, screens and framed pictures need a detector for textured planes (B-6).

### Speed
The whole pipeline, all stages, in wall-clock seconds per second of video on this machine: **median 0.49, maximum 2.15**. The maximum is a 3.2 s clip, where fixed costs dominate. These clips are 3–4 s at 480×270 or 640×360; longer and larger videos have not been measured. On Vercel's free plan (1 vCPU), a stage that runs past the function's time limit resumes in the next runner request without repeating finished stages (`test_an_analysis_resumes_after_running_out_of_time`).

## Regression protection
`tests/test_intelligence.py::test_a_lighting_drift_is_not_a_cut_but_a_real_cut_is` runs the real pipeline on five clips: `mild_lighting`, `lighting_change`, `cut_inside` and two `bare` clips. It checks the cut count, and that the configuration recorded with the analysis says which method produced it.

## What is needed for a real evaluation
1. **Licensed real footage with written permission (B-7),** labelled with cuts and surfaces. `tests/test_real_footage.py` and `benchmark/` already have the manifest, consent checks and run sheet for it.
2. **An evaluated, commercially licensed detection model (B-6)** for objects and textured surfaces, measured on that footage against the free plan's limits.
