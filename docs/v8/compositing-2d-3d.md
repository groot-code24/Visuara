# 2D and 3D compositing (V8)

Both paths produce a **plan** (`indices`, `apply(idx, frame)`, `mask(idx, shape)`). The same plan object drives the unapproved preview (`GET /api/placements/{id}/preview`), the final render (`render_plan`) and output validation (`validate_plan`). Preview/export parity therefore holds by construction.

## 2D decal (`Planar2DPlan`, app/compositor.py)
1. **Asset preparation** (`prepare_asset`, on upload): decompression and size limits (≤ 40 MP, ≤ 8000 px), EXIF orientation applied, embedded ICC profile converted to sRGB with Little CMS, RGBA derived PNG written **next to the untouched original**. The report records orientation and ICC fixes, transparency share, and an edge-halo score; it warns on `NO_TRANSPARENCY`, `LOW_RESOLUTION`, `EDGE_HALO` and `ICC_PROFILE_IGNORED`. No automatic background removal is attempted.
2. **Geometry:** unchanged from V6 (validated convex quad, aspect-fit inside the surface, per-frame homography). The tracker now re-anchors each frame to the reference surface with ECC on equal-size crops.
3. **Edges:** the product is converted to **linear light and premultiplied by alpha**. It is area-prefiltered towards its on-screen size (anti-aliasing) before the perspective warp, then composited as `bg·(1−α) + colour` in linear light. This removes the dark fringe that straight-alpha warping produced on transparent edges (regression test: minimum edge value 224 in V6 against ≥ 250 in V8, white on white).
4. **Colour integration** (`temporal_gains`): per frame, the mean linear RGB of the rectified tracked surface is divided by its value in the reference frame. The ratio is smoothed (centred median over 5 frames, then mean over 3), clamped to the quality policy's lighting range (0.75–1.33), and **not applied at all** if it stays within ±2% across the interval (measurement noise). It is applied as a per-channel gain to the product. This follows *changes* of exposure or white balance seen on the surface; it does not estimate the absolute scene illuminant. Reviewer controls `adjust.exposure_ev` and `adjust.temperature` (each ±1, bounded) are part of the versioned placement. Every applied parameter is logged in the job's validation record (`plan.color_match`).
5. **Shadows:** a flat decal lies *in* the surface, so it casts no shadow. Contact and cast shadows belong to the 3D path.
6. **Temporal behaviour:** the quality gate rejects scene cuts, drift, occlusion, blur and large lighting changes; smoothing never crosses a cut because a cut rejects the placement.

## 3D object on a tracked surface (`Tracked3DPlan`, app/render3d.py)
Inputs: a validated GLB, the four corners of the supporting surface on the reference frame, the surface's **real width and depth in metres**, anchor (u, v), yaw, scale, and light azimuth/elevation (reviewer-set; a default is flagged).

1. **GLB ingestion** (app/assets3d.py): self-contained binary glTF 2.0 only. External, data-URI or file URIs are rejected. Limits: 25 MB, 100k triangles, 4096 px textures. Accessor bounds are checked; only triangles; node transforms are applied; normals are computed if absent; metallic materials are flagged (shaded as dielectric).
2. **Camera:** the tracked surface gives four image points per frame for four known plane points. The focal length is estimated by planar calibration over up to 40 views (principal point centred, square pixels, no distortion). **Observability test:** re-solving all views with the focal ±15% must raise reprojection RMS by ≥ 0.5 px. Otherwise `FOCAL_UNOBSERVABLE`, unless the reviewer supplies the camera field of view.
3. **Pose:** IPPE planar PnP per frame. The two-fold ambiguity is resolved by error, then by rotation continuity, and flips over 15° are rejected.
4. **Placement:** glTF (+Y up, +Z front) maps to surface coordinates. The object is centred on its footprint, its lowest point is placed on the surface, then rotated by yaw and moved to the anchor. If the footprint leaves the surface: `OBJECT_OFF_SURFACE`.
5. **Rasterisation:** 2× supersampled ROI, back-face culling, faces drawn far-to-near (adequate for closed product meshes; not a general z-buffer), perspective-correct barycentric interpolation of normals and UVs, bilinear texture sampling in linear light.
6. **Shading:** albedo × (0.35 ambient + 0.75 Lambert) + Blinn specular (dielectric F0 0.04, shininess from roughness). The surface's temporal colour gains and reviewer exposure/temperature are applied.
7. **Shadows:** the cast shadow is the mesh projected onto the plane along the light direction; the contact shadow is the footprint hull expanded by 15%. Both are blurred in proportion to object size and **clipped to the tracked surface**, so they never leak beyond it. Opacity is 0.45 for the cast shadow when the light is reviewer-set and 0.25 when it is the default; 0.55 for the contact shadow.
8. **Foreground check:** after compensating the dominant (full-frame homography) camera motion, any independent change in the object's region rejects the placement. This is deliberately conservative: background parallax behind the object also triggers it (see the benchmark report).

### What 3D does not do
No light estimation from footage, no environment maps or reflections, no occlusion by scene objects, no depth estimation, no motion-blur synthesis, no multi-plane scenes, no deformable or animated models, no reconstruction of 3D models from photos.
